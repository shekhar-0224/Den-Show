# **DEN** [den.show]

_Back what you believe_

Den is a video-first creator app where fans don't just passively consume content, they actively back creators. By subscribing, tipping & engaging, fans drive discovery & reputation for creators, and get returns as they grow.

## **Den Solves**

The creator economy today is built on extractive, opaque platforms (e.g., YouTube, Twitch), where:

- Creators lose 30-50% of their revenue to centralised platforms
- Monetization is limited to ads/tips
- Fans have no upside in the creator earnings
- Reputation is owned and scored by the platform to manipulate & control distribution [recommendation engine]
- Onchain platforms like pump.fun reward fast speculation. Most tokens drop >80% in 24 hours, serving as exit liquidity for early traders. Token first, not content first.
- Only big creators win, smaller ones get zero visibility or support.

Den flips this:

- **Fair monetization:** Creators earn directly, no 30 - 50% platform cut. 80% to Creators | 15% to Fans | 5% Platform fees
- **Incentives aligned:** Fans back early and earn as creators grow.
- **Reputation is earned, not game-able:** Based on verifiable engagement, not bots.
- **Fans drive growth:** A % of Creator's earnings goes back to their fans to help expand reach & retention.
- **No hype cycles:** Value comes from quality content, growing fanbase and consistent participation.
- **Everyone starts equal:** Small creators can earn from day one — no follower threshold, no algorithm gate.
